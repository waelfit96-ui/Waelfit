How do I receive my PDF?

Right after payment you get a download link on screen and by email. If you can't find the email, check your spam folder or write to me.

Can I read it on my phone?

Yes. The PDFs work on any phone, tablet or computer, and you can print them.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and the PDF is yours to keep. For live coaching, see 1:1 sessions at €20.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Every ebook explains each exercise step by step, with photos and easier options. If you're unsure which one to pick, message me on WhatsApp.

Can I get a refund?

Because the PDF is delivered instantly, the right of withdrawal ends once the download starts (you agree to this at checkout). If there's a problem with your file, contact me and I'll fix it. See the Terms.

Is it safe if I have an injury or I'm pregnant?

Please check with your doctor before you start. The ebooks are fitness guidance, not medical advice.
