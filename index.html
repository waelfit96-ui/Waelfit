FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How do the 1:1 online sessions work?

We meet live by video call. I watch your movement, correct your technique in real time and adapt each session to how you feel that day. Each session costs €20, with no subscription: book one session or as many as you like.

Is this right for me if I'm over 50 or a beginner?

Yes. My coaching is designed for women from 27 to 70. Every exercise has easier and harder options, and as a PE teacher I'm used to teaching from the very basics.

Can I train during pregnancy?

For most healthy pregnancies, gentle exercise is recommended. I adapt every session to your trimester, and my pregnancy ebook includes clear safety rules. Please check with your doctor or midwife before you start.

Which languages do you coach in?

I coach in English, French, German and Arabic, and I schedule sessions to fit your local time zone.

Do I need equipment?

No gym needed. A mat is enough for most sessions; light dumbbells or a resistance band are a nice extra.

How can I pay?

You can pay securely with PayPal, Mastercard or any major credit or debit card.

Is there a subscription?

No. You pay per coaching session, and each ebook is a one-time payment: the PDF is yours to keep.
